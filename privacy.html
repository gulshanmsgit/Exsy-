Exsy · Money Book

Privacy policy

Last updated:

In short: your money book belongs to you. Exsy has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and never sells or shares your data. If you don’t sign in, everything stays on your phone. If you sign in with Google, your money book is stored in your own private space in Google’s Firebase database, which only your account can open.

What Exsy does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • Exsy does not read your SMS or contacts. When you use “Paste SMS”, only the text you paste or share into Exsy is read, on your device.
  • We do not see your bank or card logins – Exsy never connects to your bank.

Sharing that you choose

PDF statements and reminder messages are made on your device. They leave it only when you share them yourself (for example on WhatsApp). Backup files you download are saved where you choose.

Services Exsy uses

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) – sign-in and storage for signed-in users. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. Firebase privacy
  • Google Fonts – the app’s letters and icons are downloaded from Google, which sees your device’s IP address.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) – the PDF maker is downloaded from there the first time you make a PDF.
  • GitHub Pages – hosts the Exsy website and these pages.
  • Google Play – if you buy Exsy Premium, Google handles the payment. We never see your card or UPI details.

How long we keep data

Your money book is kept until you delete it. When you delete your account, your money book and sign-in account are removed from the database straight away; copies in Google’s own internal backups expire after a limited time. Data only on your device is removed when you use “Erase everything” or uninstall the app. Android may include app data in your phone’s encrypted backup to your own Google account.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export (More → Download backup / Export CSV) and delete your data at any time inside the app. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may also ask us to access, correct or erase your data, and raise a grievance, by writing to . We reply within 30 days.

Delete your account and data: in the app go to More → Account & devices → Delete my account and data, or follow these steps.

Security

Signed-in money books can only be opened by their own Google account (enforced by the database’s security rules). You can also set a PIN lock and hide amounts on screen. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked.

Children

Exsy is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
