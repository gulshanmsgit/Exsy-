Delete your Exsy account and data

Exsy by

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Exsy and go to More → Account & devices.
  2. Tap Delete my account and data.
  3. Confirm with your Google account. Everything is deleted straight away.

Tip: download a backup first (More → Download backup) if you may want your entries later.

Without the app

Send an email to from the Google account you use in Exsy, with the subject “Delete my Exsy account”. We delete your account and money book within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your whole money book: accounts, entries, people, chits, EMIs, budgets and settings.
  • Your Exsy sign-in account (your Google account itself is not touched).

What is kept

  • Nothing in Exsy’s database. Copies in Google’s internal backups expire after a limited time.
  • If you bought Premium, Google Play keeps its own purchase records, as required by law.

Using Exsy without signing in?

Then your data is only on your phone. Delete it with More → Erase everything, or by uninstalling the app.
